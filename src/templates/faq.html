How do I order a product?+

Open a product page and select “Ask about this product.” Contact us to confirm the price, size, availability and delivery details.

Can I request a custom design?+

Yes. Use the Custom Orders form to choose a product, describe your design and upload reference images. Each custom design is made for one customer and is not reproduced.

What can I customize?+

You can request designs for T-shirts, hoodies, artwork, bracelets and tables. Designs can include portraits, pet photos, business logos or text. Choose “Something Else” in the form for other requests.

How soon will I hear from you?+

We usually reply within 1–2 business days.

Where can I find sizes, care, and delivery details?+

Contact us with the product name. We can discuss sizes, care and shipping before you order. Full guides will be added to the site.
