A note from the founder

I started LUVIO to make clothing, accessories and artwork with original crystal designs.

The range includes crystal T-shirts, hoodies, watch bracelets and framed artwork.

I also take custom requests for photos, logos, names and other designs. You can send a reference image and tell me what product you have in mind.

I completed professional training at a specialized crystal design school and earned my Crystalizer certification.

Each crystal is placed by hand. If you have questions about a product, get in touch for sizes, materials, pricing and availability.

Thank you for taking a look at LUVIO.
